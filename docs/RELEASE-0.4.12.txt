FLIGHT WALL 0.4.12

The main cards now use the shopping ticker's crisp presentation. Both
display paths preserve the original logical artwork until the final scale.
The programmable LED shader and glow are removed. Card changes use simple
wipes without smearing or fading the artwork.

Today's Activities recognizes locally configured child initials and can
assign an unnamed activity to the configured child. A private preference
can give one activity precedence on the same day. Calendar entries stay
read-only, and personal mappings stay on the device.

Nearby aircraft route enrichment can now look up a U.S. registration when
no supported airline callsign is available. The same verified airborne
match, spending caps, and position freshness rules apply.
