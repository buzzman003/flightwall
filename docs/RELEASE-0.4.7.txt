FLIGHT WALL 0.4.7 - OPERATING MAP, TRIP WEATHER AND PORTS PREVIEW

PUBLISHED, INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED AND STAGED - ACTIVATION PENDING

DISPLAY AND FLIGHT CONTEXT

* Operating focus keeps the current flight ahead of distant trip previews in
  the configured before/after window. A bounded provider-confirmed airborne
  extension handles delays. Short household cards remain; timers and the
  shopping ticker stay live. Phone state never proves a flight is airborne.
* A high-resolution operating inset fits the route endpoints and an actual,
  fresh, identity-matched AeroAPI position, then narrows toward the destination.
  No position is interpolated from a schedule. Confirmed VPS arrival can restore
  the ordinary five-statute-mile nearby view for an hour without changing
  household presence. This does not claim continuous tracking or coverage.
* Regional OSM water/roads remain; wider views use bundled Natural Earth data
  and identify limited detail. Public OurAirports runway geometry is drawn at
  close range where available. Taxi positions need actual provider observations;
  runway references are not taxi guidance, a filed route or operational charts.
* Trip summaries and legends use airport pairs and supported flight designators
  instead of repeating city names. Existing dated VPS departures and separately
  matched Buzz leave-home clocks remain.
* Nearby aircraft show the closest three, received altitude, groundspeed and
  track, plus origin/destination only when a current airborne provider flight
  is uniquely associated. Unknown fields stay unknown. Route results age out
  after ten minutes, and the aircraft position must still be fresh.

REQUEST BUDGET AND OPTIONAL CONNECTIONS

* Operating lookups run before watches and optional nearby enrichment. All use
  the same durable $5/month nominal request-fee cap and daily request limit.
  Nearby enrichment is additionally limited to at most $1 or 20% of the monthly
  cap, 20 requests or 20% of the daily allowance, one lookup per five minutes,
  and stops when the overall budget reaches 80%. Successful/missing matches are
  cached. This leaves remaining allowance for operating flights.
* This is an application budget, not a FlightAware account-wide billing cap.
  Other clients, provider pricing changes, taxes and account terms remain
  separate. Tracking stops when the configured budget or pricebook expires.
  No provider key or real account state is included in the release.
* Position and status calls alternate on the existing polling interval; at its
  default 120 seconds each kind is normally refreshed about every four minutes.
  Network/provider delays and budget exhaustion can reduce that cadence.

WEATHER

* Optional AviationWeather.gov decoded METAR/TAF cards cover the day before,
  local flight day and bounded scheduled layovers. Airport selection is capped
  at four. Public NWS airport forecasts show today/tomorrow where supported.
  Unknown reports, uncertain TAF groups and missing temperatures stay explicit.
* Optional NOAA national radar shows a recent observation on the day before
  and day of travel. It is not a next-day forecast. Source/fetch clocks and
  freshness checks prevent an old image from looking current.
* Optional FAA airport ground-stop, ground-delay and arrival/departure-delay
  advisories are limited to the relevant airports. An unavailable feed does
  not mean no delays. These public-feed summaries are household context,
  not a dispatch briefing or a substitute for official aviation weather.
* All new provider flags default off, and DEMO makes no requests. Public airport
  queries do not contain household coordinates. Radar and FAA requests use
  fixed national URLs. Parsed caches and radar pixels stay in the private
  data directory outside releases. Home weather still refreshes automatically
  every fifteen minutes. A genuine TONIGHT-only card omits redundant missing-
  daytime-high filler; no high is invented.

PORTS AND MOTION

* Bundled logo/poster and a silent fifteen-second, 1024x512 H.264 demo preview
  can be registered in the Ports gamelist. The narrow helper backs up the XML
  and replaced Flight Wall media and preserves unrelated entries byte for byte.
  Existing frontend installations require a safe metadata reload; do not patch
  a running frontend's unsaved gamelist. Theme/video support varies.
* A rare two-second split-flap reveal joins the existing twenty-four effects.
  No sound or flashes. Privacy, backend outages and eligibility changes discard
  old pixels immediately. New weather/radar/live map cards do not enter the
  transition cache. Brightness, timer/ticker insets and software fallback apply
  to the geographic layers as well as the logical 512x256 board.
