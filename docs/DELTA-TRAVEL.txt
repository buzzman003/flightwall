PERSONAL DELTA LISTINGS
Flight Wall 0.4.21

Automatic imports are optional and start off. Chris connects read-only Gmail
separately from Google Calendar. ChatGPT's Gmail connection does not authorize
the Batocera app. Inbox previews and Lisa's Gmail are separate choices.

In household setup, open Your Google accounts. Connect Chris's read-only Gmail,
then enter the exact full passenger-name spellings used in his listings and
enable My Delta booking emails. Lisa's optional exact-name filters can also
include her bookings from Chris's mailbox; the card identifies each traveler.
The app checks about every five minutes while
the display or background service runs. It can read past listings received in
the last 30 days; past travel does not become an upcoming card.

The supported template is Non Revenue Listing from exactly no-reply@delta.com.
The app validates the message headers, passenger section and complete flight
table. It converts the printed airport-local clocks using each airport's time
zone, including DST. Unsupported dates, formats, cancellations or ambiguous
content require review instead of a guessed itinerary.

Only flight number, route, scheduled clocks, source date and listing status
are retained for display. The parser discards email bodies, employee numbers,
record locators and passenger lists. Owner-entered name filters and an opaque
salted booking association remain in private settings/storage outside releases.
No email is sent, marked read, moved or deleted by this feature.

A non-revenue listing appears as LISTED; it does not prove a confirmed seat or
actual travel. Listed, requested and confirmed personal trips keep their own
labels. They do not change the operating roster, duty, household presence or
paid flight tracking. Guest mode hides personal travel. S > Cards can hide it.

DESKTOP CLIENT SIGN-IN

If the existing Google client is a Desktop client, keep that client intact and
use the separate Gmail command. On Windows, keep this strict SSH tunnel open:

    ssh -N -L 127.0.0.1:8766:127.0.0.1:8766 -o StrictHostKeyChecking=yes root@192.168.1.173

Then, in a second SSH terminal on Batocera, run:

    /userdata/roms/ports/.data/flightwall/flightwall gmail-authorize --credentials /userdata/system/configs/flightwall/accounts/chris/google-client.json --no-browser

Open the printed Google link in the Windows browser. Select Chris's account,
review Gmail read-only permission and approve it yourself. Keep the link,
authorization codes and tokens out of chat. The loopback callback uses port
8766. The command stores a separate private Gmail client/token pair and leaves
Calendar authorization and settings unchanged. It does not enable email imports
until Chris saves the separate Delta choice and passenger-name filter.

The Google project must have Gmail API enabled. Google's read-only Gmail scope
covers mailbox read access; Flight Wall applies its narrower filters locally.
For a household-only app, Google may show an unverified-app warning. Review
your own app/account before approving. Do not change the scope to Gmail modify
or full-mail access. No password or API key is needed in chat.
