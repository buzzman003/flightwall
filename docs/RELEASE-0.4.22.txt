FLIGHT WALL 0.4.22

Bounded, cancellable TV startup readiness handles a slow existing backend
without starting another Runtime or showing cached private cards.

Nearby Aircraft adds a nearest-ten outer list when the configured inner ring
is freshly empty, prominent registration/verified flight number and aircraft
type, and small original type silhouettes where supported. Source-clock
freshness, persistent community request limits and Retry-After are enforced.
Unkeyed requests are limited to 480 per rolling day, at least 180 seconds apart.
No one-second feed, fee increase or additional provider account is provisioned.
Paid nearby route enrichment runs only with a short viewing lease from the
focused nearby card. Background/headless operation and other cards do not
consume that optional route allowance. Existing caps/cooldowns remain.

Lisa's separate Gmail profile imports supported matching Delta listings without
enabling mail previews, Calendar, presence or private cards. Optional exact-leg
tracking shares the existing FlightAware allowance and never proves boarding.

The existing crisp LED board/ticker/timer presentation, games, private settings,
calendar credentials and original pinned recovery helper must be preserved.
Release gates: full Linux tests, real OpenGL/software rendering, fresh install,
original 0.3.0 and published 0.4.21 executable upgrades and startup rollback.
Live phone, mailbox approval, future airline tracking and physical Echo behavior
require their own observed checks.
