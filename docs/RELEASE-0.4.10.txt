FLIGHT WALL 0.4.10

Family Agenda puts today's timed activities on the left and the next six days
on the right, with custody and planned household availability where known.
An explicitly selected FC View roster-note source can restore timed legs
from a supported MiCrew rotation export. It uses airport-local time zones,
retains only scheduled leg/report fields, and preserves deadhead markings.
No separate MiCrew account is needed when FC View has the supported notes.
Detailed nearby geography extends from the local map to the Gulf Coast at
10, 25, 50 and 100 miles. The map remains crisp with LED effects on the board.
Verified nearby routes can display IATA or ICAO airport codes.

Alexa has more tomorrow dinner and named timer phrases. Unclear day requests
get a clarification rather than a silent date switch. Saying cancel at a
dinner prompt stops the conversation. Use “Alexa, open Flight Wall skill”
or “Alexa, ask Flight Wall to set tomorrow's dinner to lasagna.”

Selecting a generated meal can show its existing picture and email its full
recipe when the owner configures a local Gmail app password. No public or
remote settings accept mail destinations. SMS requires separate setup.

The optional flightwall_autostart service opens the normal Ports entry once
per boot. Escape returns to the games menu. The signed update preserves
existing settings, games, trusted keys and the original pinned recovery helper.
