FLIGHT WALL 0.4.6 - NAVIGATION, RETRO REVEALS AND WEATHER CLOCKS

PUBLISHED, INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED AND ACTIVE

* Left/right selects the previous/next eligible card, wrapping at either end.
  Keyboard arrows, controller horizontal D-pad/left stick and remotes that emit
  arrow keys are supported. Stick movement is edge-triggered to avoid rapid
  repeats. Settings retain directional adjustments. Actual device mapping and
  TV navigation still need physical acceptance.
* Twenty-four quarter-second retro reveals add movement without bright flashes
  or glow. The software fallback remains. Eligibility/config/guest/backend
  changes discard old pixels immediately. Message/artwork/health/hotel and
  weather/nearby/watch views do not enter the cache. Timers and shopping ticker
  stay live outside effects. Exact dinner/live-position cutoff changes cancel
  effects; high-resolution map layers attach only after a reveal finishes.
* Weather shows NWS ISSUED separately from CHECKED. The automatic 15-minute poll
  remains. Two actual retrievals occurred 15 minutes 1 second apart while 0.4.5
  stayed open. An evening forecast can legitimately lack the daytime high;
  TONIGHT/LOW and DAY HIGH NOT PROVIDED explain this. No past high is guessed.
* Canonical ASCII/LF VERSION validation runs before building. Installer semver,
  signed archive checks, monotonic floor, next-launch activation, startup
  rollback and original pinned 0.3.0 recovery remain intact.
* AeroAPI is a separate optional connection. Personal currently has no monthly
  minimum and up to $5 free monthly usage. The app caps nominal lookup/position
  request fees at $5/month, shared with watches. It does not cap provider-wide
  usage, other clients or taxes/fees. No key is included and no positive paid
  provider result is claimed. Account/key setup remains a separate user step.
