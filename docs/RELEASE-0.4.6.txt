FLIGHT WALL 0.4.6 - NAVIGATION, RETRO REVEALS AND WEATHER CLOCKS

SOURCE CANDIDATE - NOT YET A PUBLISHED EXECUTABLE

* Left/right selects the previous/next currently eligible card, wrapping at
  either end. Keyboard and remotes that emit arrow keys are supported, along
  with controller horizontal D-pad and left stick. Settings keep their existing
  directional controls. Physical controller/remote mapping needs device checks.
* Twenty-four brief retro reveals add card movement without bright flashes or
  glow. The software display fallback remains. Privacy/expiry/guest/backend
  changes discard previous pixels immediately. Timer and ticker overlays keep
  updating outside the card transition; no transition extends a private card.
* Weather labels NWS issuance separately from its last successful retrieval.
  The 15-minute automatic poll remains. After the daytime period disappears from
  the NWS forecast, the card can show TONIGHT and its supplied low, with an
  explicit missing-daytime-high explanation. No historical high is invented.
* All private data stays outside releases. Signed next-launch activation,
  monotonic version floor, startup rollback, original pinned 0.3.0 recovery and
  the software rendering path remain required gates. Native upgrade validation
  uses original 0.3.0 and published 0.4.5 executables.
* AeroAPI remains a separate optional connection. The Personal tier currently
  has no monthly minimum and up to $5 monthly free query usage. The app limits
  nominal lookup/position fees to at most $5/month, shared with flight watches.
  This does not impose an account-wide provider spending cap or cover other
  clients/taxes/fees. No API key is included in this release.
