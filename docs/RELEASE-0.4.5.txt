FLIGHT WALL 0.4.5 - DECK PROGRESS, AM/PM, WEATHER AND FLIGHT STATUS

PUBLISHED, VERIFIED AND STAGED - NEXT SAFE PORTS LAUNCH

* Progress uses the actual per-frame eligible rotation, left-to-right, rather
  than a fixed list of view types. Individual trip pages and priority repeats
  are real slots. Hidden private cards do not contribute counts. Normal decks
  show all squares; large decks use bounded 24-square pages and index/total.
* Every TV clock is 12-hour AM/PM with existing Central/DST/date semantics.
  Stored RFC3339 timestamps, durations and exact visibility windows stay intact.
* NWS polling changes from30 to 15 minutes, automatically in the independent
  worker while the display OR background service is running. There is no need
  to close/reopen the display. Fresh received summaries have small crisp icons;
  stale/unknown forecasts retain honest labels and a question mark.
* Operating-status polling prepares actual/estimated gate-out/in or runway-
  off/on clocks, early/late comparisons against the SAME provider event's
  scheduled baseline, and diversion/tracking-ended notices. Schedule is never
  relabeled as live data. Tracking-ended does not prove airline cancellation.
* One date/route/canonical-ident provider match is required, followed by its
  exact provider flight ID for positions. Ambiguous results never select a
  nearest flight. Queries are bounded to4 hours before..6 hours after the leg;
  a departed flight can outlast its roster arrival, without inferring presence.
* Default 120-second polling alternates provider status/position after initial
  association, so each normally refreshes about4 minutes while eligible. Status
  expires after5 minutes and is rechecked every frame, including pause/outage.
  No estimate or early/late verdict is displayed as current when stale.
* The owner chose to connect AeroAPI later. No key/account was configured and
  no paid API call was made. This release supplies capability, not live-data
  activation. Existing FC View and Buzz schedule views remain available.
* flightaware_monthly_limit_usd defaults 5 and is local-only, capped at 5. Shared
  operating/watch calls reserve full published nominal fees BEFORE network I/O,
  under an interprocess lock, in private flightaware-usage.json. Status lookup
  costs $0.005/result-set, position$0.010; max_pages=1 enforced. Daily 240-request
  limit remains. Uncertain/failed calls are not refunded. UTC month rollover,
  malformed ledgers, persistence failure and concurrent processes are covered.
* This is the app's request budget at checked prices, not a provider-wide hard
  spending cap; other account usage, taxes/fees and credits are not controlled.
  Rates were checked October 31, 2026; after 90 days, paid calls stop pending rate
  review. A legacy daily-only ledger conservatively consumes that old month's
  full allowance, because it cannot establish earlier monthly spending.
* Private credentials, caches, settings, original recovery helper and games
  remain outside versioned releases. Native gates use original 0.3.0 and
  published 0.4.4 executable upgrade/rollback paths. GUI-live staging takes
  coherent individual-file backups and keeps the current display running;
  activation remains next-launch only.
