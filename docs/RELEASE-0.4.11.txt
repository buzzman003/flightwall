FLIGHT WALL 0.4.11

The family outlook again shows ten days of Mom's House, Dad's House,
change days and unknown days. Today's Activities is a separate card
for the selected family calendar. It recognizes jiu-jitsu, soccer,
dance, gymnastics, viola, violin and configured aliases. It shows the
calendar's own times and all-day entries, with extra pages on busy days.
Guest mode and stale or unavailable data hide activity details.
The OpenAI API is not used to process family calendar events.

LED dots now use crisp, whole-screen-pixel gaps in both the OpenGL
and software renderers. Glow is off by default on new installations.
Saved display preferences are preserved by the update.
