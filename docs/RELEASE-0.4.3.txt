FLIGHT WALL 0.4.3 - CLEAR MAPS, VPS TIMES AND DINNER NOTICE

SOURCE CANDIDATE

The installed 0.4.2 release remains unchanged until a signed 0.4.3 executable
has passed the Linux/native workflow, been verified and activated at a normal
Ports launch. Local edits do not update the Batocera executable or Alexa model.

* Trip/leg geographic layers no longer draw airport-code labels over routes.
  Date colors, endpoint markers and the dated city-pair legend remain.
* An inbound VPS row with an exact immediate outbound now shows the outbound
  date and Central clock, in addition to its planned stay. A matching layover
  card already has the full dated departure; the update makes it easy to find
  on the upcoming-trip schedule too.
* Optional brief_leave_times_enabled defaults false and is local-only.
  Read descriptions transiently only from Chris's selected secondary calendar;
  retain labeled recommended/report-safe timestamps from the confirmed VPS
  Home Overnight format, with arrival/report anchors. Require exact matching
  inbound arrival, immediate operating outbound and report. Unknown formats,
  invalid civil times and ambiguous matches do not supply a leave time.
  No original note, personal message, crew list or contact is cached.
* brief_leave_policy defaults conflict: distinct values produce a check-time
  warning rather than a selected clock. Owner-selected report_safe/recommended
  policies display that labeled source clock while retaining VERIFY REPORT.
  This feature reads planning notes; it does not calculate or guarantee an
  operationally correct departure-from-home time.
* Dinner keeps its configured window (default14:30-19:00, end-exclusive).
  Each displayed frame uses the household local clock: green SNACKS OK before
  17:00, red NO MORE SNACKS from17:00. Custody/privacy and appointments retain
  their existing rules. Side dishes can be part of the spoken meal phrase.
* The nearby map draws real bundled regional OSM shoreline, water, roads,
  US98 and runway geometry. It has no airport-name labels. The geographic
  patch bypasses LED dots at TV presentation resolution; titles/details retain
  the normal board style. The patch follows timer/ticker animation and honors
  brightness in OpenGL and software fallback. Missing/out-of-region data keeps
  the existing position plot. The saved center never enters status/MCP JSON.
* The regional map download uses a fixed public city-region bounding box,
  never the household point. OSM attribution and ODbL apply to the database;
  its source, date and preparation script ship with the source package.
  Aircraft remain received fresh positions within the configured radius,
  not interpolated tracks. No paid provider or route enrichment is enabled.
* Additional Alexa dinner samples use the existing skill intents. Physical
  Echo discovery remains unresolved and requires an Amazon/account routing
  investigation; model edits alone cannot promise to fix it.

VALIDATION

Source tests and visual previews are recorded in docs/VALIDATION.txt. Native
release gates use original0.3.0 and published0.4.2 executables, preserving the
original0.3.0 recovery helper, private settings/credentials and unrelated games.
No new physical TV, phone, paid-provider or Alexa speech success is implied.
