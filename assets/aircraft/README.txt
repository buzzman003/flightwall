FLIGHT WALL AIRCRAFT PHOTOS

Each JPEG is a stock photo indexed by an ICAO aircraft type designator. The
display uses the type reported by the aircraft feed. Liveries and registrations
in the photographs are examples from the source photos. Some designators
cover related variants. D328 uses a C-146A Wolfhound photographed at Hurlburt
Field and F900 uses a representative Falcon 900.

ATTRIBUTION.tsv records the original title, creator, source, license and
changes for each JPEG. Each photograph retains its individual license,
including the applicable ShareAlike license for adapted CC BY-SA photos.
Flickr images were discovered with Openverse and their licenses were checked
on the original Flickr pages. The public-domain military photographs are
credited to their named photographers and the applicable service.

The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not
imply or constitute DoW endorsement.
