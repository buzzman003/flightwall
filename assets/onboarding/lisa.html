Allow background updates

  1. In iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → OwnTracks, choose Always when available and enable Precise Location. iOS may ask in two steps after first opening the app.
  2. Allow OwnTracks to use cellular data and Background App Refresh. Low Power Mode can pause background refresh; keep it off for this initial test.
  3. In OwnTracks, choose Significant monitoring. This saves battery, but updates depend on movement and are not immediate.
  4. Send/publish your current location from OwnTracks, then return here and check the connection.
